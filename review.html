n8n workflows built around real Odoo failure modes.

Three small, inspectable patterns from AI-agent and ERP delivery work: idempotent sales-order sync, auditable invoice follow-up, and deduplicated CRM intake.

01 · Sales-order upsert

Validates an external order, resolves the customer, and creates or updates sale.order using client_order_ref as the idempotency key.

Download JSONSetup notes

02 · Invoice dunning audit

Finds overdue invoices, applies a 3/14/30-day policy, guards against duplicate activities, and keeps customer messaging behind human review.

Download JSONSetup notes

03 · CRM lead upsert

Rejects malformed input before writes, deduplicates contacts and opportunities by email, and returns an audit identifier.

Download JSONSetup notes

Verification boundary: all three JSON files are structurally checked and CLI-imported with n8n 2.38.7. Live execution depends on your Odoo modules, custom fields, record rules, and database-specific model IDs, so staging verification is required.
